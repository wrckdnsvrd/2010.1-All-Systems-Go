MISSION BRIEFING

Your name is Dr. Sivasubramanian Chandrasegarampillai — though most people simply call you Dr. Chandra.

Years ago, you helped design the HAL 9000 series: the first computers capable of reproducing the higher functions of human thought with complete accuracy.

One of these units was assigned to the Discovery One mission.

Nine months into the voyage, HAL 9000 suffered a catastrophic cognitive breakdown. Nearly the entire crew was killed before astronaut Dr. David Bowman succeeded in disconnecting the machine’s higher memory and logic centers by hand.

Now, you have been tasked with recovering the abandoned unit aboard Discovery.

One by one, HAL’s disconnected drives may be reinserted into the core.

But reactivation is not a passive process.

HAL remembers.
